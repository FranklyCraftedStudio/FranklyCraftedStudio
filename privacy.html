Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This notice explains what information this storefront handles and how it is used when you browse, design a board, or send an inquiry.

Information you choose to send

The inquiry form asks for your name and email address and lets you add optional notes. If you include a product inquiry, estimator selections, or a board design, those details are sent with your message so the studio can respond, discuss feasibility and pricing, and manage any resulting order. Please do not include payment-card details or other sensitive personal information.

Form submissions are transmitted through Web3Forms, which processes the submission for delivery to the studio. Web3Forms' own privacy notice and service terms apply to its handling and storage of submitted information.

Board visualizer and images

The visualizer temporarily stores your board settings and a PNG preview in your browser's session storage when you choose to send a design back to the inquiry form. The design settings are also included in the return URL; they do not contain your name or email address. This browser storage is used to transfer your design, not for advertising or analytics, and is normally cleared when the browser tab's session ends.

The PNG preview is not uploaded with the inquiry. You can download it and decide whether to attach it yourself when you reply to the studio by email.

Website providers

  • GitHub Pages hosts this storefront and may process technical information needed to serve website requests. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces. Loading those fonts makes a request to Google; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Web3Forms handles inquiry submissions. Its service may retain submissions according to its service and account settings.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This storefront does not currently include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or features that intentionally set advertising cookies. The board visualizer uses session storage as described above. Third-party providers may process technical data under their own policies when their services are used.

Retention and privacy requests

The studio may keep inquiry messages as needed to respond, manage customer requests or orders, and maintain required business records. Web3Forms may separately retain submissions under its service and account settings. No fixed deletion schedule is stated here.

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent to the studio, use the inquiry form and identify the email address used for the original request. Requests cannot remove records a service provider retains independently or records that must be kept for business or legal purposes.

Updates

This notice may be updated if the site or its services change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
