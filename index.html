Heirloom Board Care

Proper care ensures hardwood kitchenware lasts for decades.

Hand Wash Only

Never submerge in water or place in a dishwasher. Wash gently with warm water and mild soap, then towel dry immediately.

Mineral Oil Baths

When the timber begins to look dull or dry (every 3–4 weeks), apply pure food-grade mineral oil liberally and let it soak overnight.

Beeswax Sealing

Top with a wood conditioner or beeswax butter to create a moisture-resistant barrier that protects against knife scoring.
