Heirloom Board Care

How to protect your hardwood boards for decades of daily service.

1. Wash Promptly

Hand wash with warm water and mild soap. Never submerge in water or place in a dishwasher, as prolonged moisture warps grain structure.

2. Dry Upright

Towel dry immediately after washing and rest the board on its edge to allow equal airflow across both sides during drying.

3. Recondition

Apply pure food-grade mineral oil every 3–4 weeks. Lock in luster with a natural beeswax butter once the surface begins to look dry or light.
