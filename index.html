Artisan Maintenance

How to Preserve Heirloom Hardwood

Treated with care, an authentic end-grain or edge-grain cutting board will outlive generations. Follow our workshop's four golden rules.

Never Submerge or Dishwash

High heat and water saturation destroy wood fiber joints and degrade food-safe glue lines. Always hand-wipe with warm soapy water and dry immediately standing upright.

Rule 01 • Zero Soaking

Monthly Mineral Oil Bath

When timber looks pale or dry, saturate with pure food-grade mineral oil. Let soak overnight and buff away excess. This locks out bacteria and prevents warp.

Rule 02 • Hydration Routine

Seal with Beeswax Butter

Our Frankly Crafted Board Butter combines triple-filtered organic beeswax with mineral oil to establish a natural hydrophobic barrier against juices and stains.

Rule 03 • Surface Armor

Sanitize with Salt & Lemon

To eliminate stubborn cooking odors (garlic, onion, fish), scour with coarse kosher salt and a cut lemon half. Rinse lightly, pat dry, and re-oil the surface.

The Frankly Crafted Story

Honest Craftsmanship for the Modern Home.

Founded in 2026 by an artisan woodworker and designer, Frankly Crafted Studio was created with a clear philosophy: your kitchen counters, nursery walls, and living areas deserve pieces that carry soul, weight, and uncompromising integrity.

We don't manufacture cheap fast-furniture or mass-stamped cutting boards. Every batch begins with carefully selected, kiln-dried North American hardwoods—deep Black Walnut, rock-solid Hard Maple, vibrant Cherry, and exotic Purpleheart. We align grain profiles to prevent warping and finish each surface with our proprietary organic oil and beeswax formula.

What sets our studio apart is the seamless integration of modern 3D fabrication. By combining CAD modeling and high-precision additive manufacturing with traditional woodworking, we produce multi-dimensional nursery signs, bespoke brass-accented joinery, and specialized tools that push maker art forward.
