Longevity & Preservation

Heirloom Board Care

Solid hardwoods will last generations when cared for properly. Follow these four foundational guidelines.

1

Hand Wash Only

Never submerge in water or place in the dishwasher. Wash promptly with warm water and mild soap after each use, then towel dry completely.

2

Dry Vertically

Always allow the board to dry on its side or resting on its silicone feet so air circulates equally around both faces to avoid uneven moisture bowing.

3

Condition Monthly

When the timber appears dry, apply food-grade mineral oil generously, allow it to absorb overnight, and buff with beeswax wood butter.

4

Avoid Direct Heat

Keep your board away from stovetops, direct radiator heat, or damp window sills. Steady indoor humidity preserves tight glue joints.
